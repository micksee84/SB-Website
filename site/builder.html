Three ways to make

Worksheets

Drag, drop, print

Split the page into spaces and fill each one with an activity. Put their name on it, swap any picture, and print.

  • Five layouts, from one big activity to four quarters
  • Bubble letters with numbered strokes for tracing
  • Three ready-made worksheets to start from

Flash cards

A deck for every letter

Pick a letter and the cards fill with pictures that start with it. Swap any picture from its menu.

  • Letters, numbers, picture words, feelings and shapes
  • Big, medium or small: 4, 6 or 9 to a page
  • Fold-over cards with the word on the back

Planner

The day, in pictures

Lay out a morning, a week or a trip so little ones always know what comes next.

  • Day, week, checklist or first–then board
  • Ready-made checklists, like Pack for the beach
  • Make your own steps with your words and any icon

Questions

Good to know

New to the builder? Read Getting started.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes. The free plan gives you 8 worksheets a month and the planner. Saving and opening your work is always free.

What counts toward the 8 worksheets?+

Only printing or downloading a worksheet. Saving, opening and editing never count.

Does my child need an account?+

No. Accounts are for grown-ups, and children never sign in. There are no ads.

How do I sign in?+

With your email. We send you a code to type in, or a link to tap. There is no password to remember.

What do I need to print?+

Any printer and regular paper. You can also download a PDF and print it later.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, any time. Founding members who join in December keep $3.99 CAD a month for as long as they stay.
