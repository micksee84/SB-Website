Getting started

From sign-in to the printed page

A quick tour of the Worksheet Builder, in eight short steps. Read it top to bottom, or jump to the part you need.

1Sign in 2Start something 3Find your way 4Worksheets 5Flash cards 6Planner 7Save and print 8Shortcuts
1

Step 1 of 8

Sign in

Sign in with your email address. We email you a code: type it in, or tap the link in the email.

  • There's no password to remember.
  • Accounts are for grown-ups. Children never sign in.
  • Your saved work follows your account to any device.

Tip: No email? Check your spam or promotions folder, or ask for a new code.

2

Step 2 of 8

Start something new

Click New and choose what you're making today.

  • Worksheet: activity blocks such as tracing, counting and mazes.
  • Planner: a day, a week, a checklist or a first–then board, in pictures.
  • Flash cards: letters, numbers, picture words, feelings and shapes.

Tip: One document can hold all three. Add the other kinds later with Add page.

3

Step 3 of 8

Find your way around

Every kind of page uses the same layout. The numbers match the picture.

  1. Top bar: New, Open, Save and Templates, with undo, redo and shortcuts.
  2. Activity list: everything you can add, grouped by skill, with search.
  3. Pages: move, copy or delete a page, or add one.
  4. Page toolbar: layout, paper size, picture style and zoom.
  5. The page: this is what prints.
  6. Share copies a link. Print / PDF downloads a PDF or prints.

Tip: Fold the side panels away with their double-arrow buttons for more room.

4

Step 4 of 8

Make a worksheet

A worksheet page is split into spaces, and each space holds one activity.

  1. Drag an activity onto a space, or tap + to fill the next empty one.
  2. Pick a layout: 1 Full, 2 Halves, 4 Quarters, 1/2 + 2/4 or 2/4 + 1/2.
  3. Click an activity on the page and press Enter to open its settings.
  4. Change what it shows, like the letter to trace, then click Done.

Tip: There are 22 activities in seven groups. Templates has three ready-made worksheets to start from.

5

Step 5 of 8

Make flash cards

Choose Flash cards from New. The page starts with a deck for the letter A.

  1. Kind of cards: letters, numbers, picture words, feelings or shapes.
  2. Pick the letter for this page.
  3. Card size: Big (4 a page), Medium (6) or Small (9).
  4. Fold-over prints the back beside the front, to fold. One-sided puts both on one face.
  5. Swap the picture on any card from its menu.

Tip: Add a page for B starts the next letter in the deck.

6

Step 6 of 8

Plan the day

Choose Planner from New, then pick a day, a week, a checklist or a first–then board. Switch any time and your activities stay.

  1. Routine activities: drag one to its time, or tap + to add it after the last.
  2. Make your own: your words, any icon.
  3. Switch between Day, Week, Checklist and First–then.
  4. Settings: when the day starts and ends, times, tick boxes, and a name and date line.

Tip: Checklists come with ready-made lists, like Pack for the beach and Snow day.

7

Step 7 of 8

Save, print and share

Click Save and give your work a name. After that, your changes save on their own.

  • Open shows everything you've saved. Rename it with the pencil.
  • Download a PDF gives you a file with sharp lines.
  • Print now opens your browser's print window.
  • Share copies a link to your document.

Tip: Saving is always free. On the free plan, only printing counts toward your 8 worksheets a month.

8

Step 8 of 8

Tips and shortcuts

Press ? in the builder to see every shortcut.

  • Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy an activity into the next empty space.
  • Ctrl+D duplicates the selected activity, or the page.
  • Ctrl+S saves and Ctrl+P prints.
  • / jumps to the activity search.

Tip: On a Mac, use Cmd where it says Ctrl.
